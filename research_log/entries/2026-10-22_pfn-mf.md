## §1 Prior runtime and launch plan (2026-10-22)

The MF prior generates fresh episodes every step (ifBO presampled them). Profile on ulysses (one core,
batch 64, 256 query tokens, n_train ~ U[1, 100], 3 hyperparameters; scratch script, numbers in
`research_log/tasks/2026-10-22_pfn_mf.md`): `MFPrior` 1.28 s per batch, against 45 ms for a pfn_large-sized
bf16 step. Two thirds of it was the per-curve evaluation in `parse_allocation_into_sequence`.
- `priors/mf/fast.py::FastMFPrior`: the same draws in the same order, the deterministic work batched over
  all tasks (one linker call with tabulated ppfs, one curve-mixture evaluation, a lean latent MLP):
  0.39 s per batch, episodes bit-identical to MFPrior's (tested).
- `exact_stream=false`: no throwaway init draws, latent BNN only on the configurations the episode uses:
  0.24 s, the same distribution (numpy draws identical, y distribution tested), a different torch stream.
- `training/prefetch.py::PrefetchTrainer`: batches made ahead in worker processes, a pure function of
  (seed, step, n_train); identical training for 1 and 3 workers (test) and for 8, 10 and 12 workers (probe).
- Probe of `experiment=pfn_mf` on ulysses (420 steps, fitted timing): 55 ms/step with 8 workers (exact
  stream with 10 workers: 67 ms/step). The prior then adds ~10 ms to the 45 ms GPU step.
